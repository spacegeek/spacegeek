# SpaceGeek

I am an independent researcher and systems designer with professional experience in aerospace quality, nondestructive testing, electronics, manufacturing, corporate facilities, industrial maintenance, safety and operations.

My work is concerned with environments where reliable records, technical judgment, equipment condition and organizational continuity must remain connected. Experience in aerospace inspection, maintenance, facilities and engineering operations has shaped my interest in how organizations preserve evidence, decisions and practical knowledge over time.

I publish and develop online as **SpaceGeek**, a name reflecting my longstanding interest in space science, aerospace engineering and exploration.

## Current research

My current research includes:

* Provenance, evidence and decision history
* Digital preservation and records continuity
* Operational knowledge retention
* Technical terminology and semantic relationships
* Maintenance, incident and inspection records
* Operational interfaces and changing system state
* Explainable information retrieval
* Responsible evaluation of artificial intelligence and language-model systems
* Standards, assurance and practical adoption boundaries

## Professional background

My background includes aerospace quality and employer-specific nondestructive-testing qualifications in magnetic particle, eddy current, ultrasonic and visual inspection. It also includes electronics, automated material handling, manufacturing, facilities management, industrial maintenance, safety and engineering operations.

I am currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in business and continuing my professional development in quality, facilities, operations and applied research.
