## ConSERVA

My primary project is **ConSERVA**, a research and software initiative focused on provenance, digital preservation and the relationships among records, objects, observations, evidence and decisions.

The project explores how information systems can preserve context as records evolve, conclusions change and new evidence becomes available. It is intended to support a wide range of settings, including personal collections, workshops, industrial operations, archives, laboratories and institutions.

Current research includes provenance and evidence modeling, digital preservation, archival and recordkeeping standards, semantic relationships, uncertain identity, operational knowledge retention, explainable information retrieval and the evaluation of artificial intelligence and language-model systems.
