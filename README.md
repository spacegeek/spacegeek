# Chris Beisler · SpaceGeek

I am an independent researcher, systems designer, and operations professional working on problems involving provenance, digital preservation, semantic relationships, evidence, uncertainty, and durable knowledge systems.

My background includes aerospace quality and nondestructive testing, electronics, industrial maintenance, manufacturing, corporate facilities, engineering supervision, safety, and technical operations. These environments have given me direct experience with the consequences of lost context, incomplete equipment histories, undocumented decisions, incompatible systems, fragmented records, and knowledge that disappears when people, vendors, or organizations change.

I publish and develop online as **SpaceGeek**, reflecting a lifelong interest in space, aerospace science, engineering, exploration, and the systems required to preserve dependable knowledge across long periods of time.

## ConSERVA

My primary project is **ConSERVA**, a cross-domain research and software initiative exploring how records, physical and digital objects, observations, assertions, evidence, relationships, decisions, and revisions can be represented without discarding uncertainty or historical context.

ConSERVA is intended to support uses ranging from personal collections and small workshops to industrial operations, archives, laboratories, businesses, and large institutions. Its development is guided by established standards and research where appropriate, while identifying areas in which current systems or models remain incomplete.
